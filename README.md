# FUTURE_DS_01

### Project Overview

This project focused on analyzing retail sales data to evaluate business performance and identify growth opportunities. The analysis aimed to identify top revenue-generating products, evaluate sales performance across different time periods, determine high-performing product categories and regions, and provide data-driven business insights.

The project also explored customer purchasing behavior, sales trends, order performance, and regional activity to support strategic decision-making.

### Project Objectives

The main objectives of the project were to:
1. Identify the top revenue-generating products and categories
2. Analyze sales performance across different periods
3. Determine the highest revenue-performing regions
4. Understand customer purchasing frequency and spending behavior
5. Compare successful and cancelled orders across regions
6. Discover business growth opportunities using data-driven insights

### Tasks Performed

The following tasks were completed during the project:

### Key Insights

The analysis revealed several important business insights:

1. The business generated a total revenue of 560,157
2. The UK dominated the customer base and revenue contribution
3. Midday purchasing activity generated the highest revenue
4. Weekday sales significantly outperformed weekend sales
5. The Other category was the largest revenue contributor
6. Bags & Storage provided a strong secondary revenue contribution
7. Sales performance was strongest between May and November
8. Europe experienced slightly higher cancellation rates
9. Some product categories contributed very little revenue
10. Revenue was heavily dependent on one category and region, creating concentration risk

### Recommendations
Based on the findings, the following recommendations were made:

1. Maximize midday sales through targeted promotions and sufficient inventory during peak hours
2. Improve evening sales performance using discounts, campaigns, and operational optimization
3. Reduce dependency on the “Other” category by diversifying product offerings
4. Strengthen secondary categories such as Bags & Storage
5. Revive underperforming categories like Accessories and Toys & Kids through promotions and bundling
6. Leverage seasonal demand by increasing inventory and marketing during high-performing months
7. Increase weekend sales through weekend-exclusive campaigns and loyalty incentives
8. Expand growth opportunities beyond the UK market, especially in Europe
9. Convert moderate and occasional buyers into high-value customers using loyalty programmes and personalized offers
10. Investigate Europe’s higher cancellation rates and improve fulfillment and delivery processes

### Conclusion

The business demonstrates strong overall performance with total revenue of 560,157, supported by strong UK market performance, weekday sales, and midday purchasing activity.

The analysis highlighted the importance of the “Other” category as the primary revenue driver, while Bags & Storage provided consistent secondary support. Seasonal demand patterns between May and November also revealed strong opportunities for strategic inventory planning and revenue optimization.

However, the business also faces several risks and improvement areas:
Heavy dependence on one category and one region
Weak evening and weekend sales performance
Underperforming product categories
Higher cancellation rates in Europe

Overall, the business is in a strong position for future growth. By diversifying revenue streams, improving low-performing areas, strengthening customer retention strategies, and expanding beyond the UK market, the company can achieve more balanced, sustainable, and profitable growth.
